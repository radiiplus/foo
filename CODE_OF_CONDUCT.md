# Code of Conduct

FOO is built in public by people with different backgrounds, experience levels,
and viewpoints. Everyone participating in this repository is expected to make
that work direct, technical, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person presenting them.
- Give concrete evidence for technical claims and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Make space for questions, corrections, and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, consent, identities, and personal boundaries.
- Accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Insults, intimidation, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information or credentials without permission.
- Abusing issue reports, reviews, or project infrastructure.
- Retaliating against someone who raises a conduct or security concern.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release channels, and public spaces where someone represents the FOO project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately
through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not publish
sensitive personal details in an issue. Security vulnerabilities must follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions
consider the behavior's impact, severity, repetition, and response after notice.
Reports will be handled as privately as the available channel permits. A person
who is directly involved in a report should not make the final moderation
decision alone when another maintainer is available.
